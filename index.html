230+Items Tracked

Built for Alya’s Nail Art and Eyelash Extension Salon (an Ayumi franchise founded May 10, 2025 in Pallocan Kanluran, Batangas City). Previously, staff tracked over 230 inventory items manually using spreadsheets and notebooks—causing overlooked low-stock consumables and service delays during 1–2 week online restocks.

  • Centralized appointment scheduling and automated service notifications.
  • Real-time inventory deduction and low-stock monitoring to prevent stockouts.
  • Image-processing features for nail and lash design visualization.

Impact: Eliminates manual estimation between bookings and stock levels, ensuring essential consumables are replenished before running out while improving customer booking convenience.
